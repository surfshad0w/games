# Generated asset prompts

These prompts produced the project-bound Fireline thumbnail and Ara Games app icon. The existing game atlases remain the source artwork; the small JPEGs in `assets/thumbs/` are derived exports used by the hub.

## Fireline Rescue thumbnail

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: game hub thumbnail, 1536×1024 landscape. Primary request: Create a cheerful game-card illustration for a children’s iPad arcade game called Fireline Rescue. Scene/backdrop: a stylized top-down park or woodland clearing with a few simple log and rounded rock obstacles. Subject: a friendly firefighter on the left spraying a bright curved stream of water toward three stylized flames on the right, with sparkling droplets and soft steam. Style/medium: polished rounded 3D sticker-toy illustration matching a colorful family arcade, clean shapes, saturated coral, golden yellow, teal, sky blue, and warm green. Composition/framing: clear left-to-right action, bold silhouettes and strong focal separation, readable when reduced to 320×200, generous edge padding. Lighting/mood: bright, playful, safe, optimistic. Constraints: no text, letters, logos, UI, border, watermark, injury, photorealism, frightening fire, dense smoke, or cropped characters.
```

## Ara Games maskable icon

```text
Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: maskable PWA app icon, 1024×1024. Primary request: Create a joyful children’s arcade app icon for Ara Games. Subject: a large rounded golden star combined with a simple purple game controller, with tiny gem, rocket, puppy-paw, and rainbow accents around it. Style/medium: polished soft 3D sticker-toy rendering, bold silhouette, high contrast. Scene/backdrop: saturated blue-to-violet arcade gradient. Composition/framing: centered icon with all important artwork inside the central 70% maskable safe zone, readable at 48 pixels. Lighting/mood: bright, playful, optimistic. Constraints: no text, letters, border, watermark, realistic imagery, or tiny unreadable detail.
```
